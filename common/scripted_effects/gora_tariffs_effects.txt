# GORA UI+ — Bulk tariffs: scripted effects
#
# Two parameterized helpers do the heavy lifting (each iterates all 53
# vanilla goods and sets the matching tariff level), and 7+7 thin wrappers
# bind a specific LEVEL argument so the scripted_guis can call them by name.
# A 3-month cooldown variable is set after every bulk change, blocking
# rapid re-application.
#
# Goods list mirrors vanilla 1.13.4 — every entry under common/goods/ that
# actually appears on the trade panel.
